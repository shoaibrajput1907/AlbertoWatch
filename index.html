Innovation & Craft

THE TECHNOLOGY

The artistry and engineering behind every timepiece

Automatic Movement

Self-winding mechanical movements powered by the natural motion of the wearer's wrist. Hundreds of precision-engineered components work in harmony, requiring no battery — only the kinetic energy of daily life. A masterpiece of micro-engineering perfected over centuries.

Quartz Precision

Quartz crystal oscillators vibrate at precisely 32,768 Hz, delivering accuracy within seconds per month. This revolutionary technology ensures that every moment is measured with unwavering precision, combining reliability with elegance.

Eco-Drive Technology

Light-powered technology that converts any light source — natural or artificial — into energy. A single full charge can power the watch for months in complete darkness. Never needs a battery change, blending sustainability with luxury.

Smart Watch Technology

Advanced sensors, AMOLED displays, and powerful processors merge traditional watchmaking aesthetics with modern connectivity. Health monitoring, notifications, GPS tracking, and countless apps — all from your wrist.

Sapphire Crystal

Scoring 9 on the Mohs hardness scale — second only to diamond — lab-grown sapphire crystal provides virtually scratch-proof protection. Anti-reflective coatings ensure crystal-clear legibility from every angle, preserving the dial's beauty for decades.

Water Resistance

Multi-layered sealing systems with screw-down crowns and case backs ensure protection from 30 metres of everyday splash resistance to 300+ metres for professional diving. Rigorous pressure testing guarantees reliability in the most demanding conditions.

ABOUT ALBERTO

Alberto Watch Company is dedicated to providing exceptional timepieces for those who appreciate the art of horology. We specialize in luxury watches, vintage timepieces, and modern smart watches — offering retail, expert repair, and professional appraisal services.

Our commitment to customer service ensures that every interaction reflects the same standard of excellence found in the timepieces we represent.

Luxury Retail

Curated collections of the world's finest timepieces, from heritage luxury brands to modern innovations. Expert guidance for every purchase.

Watch Repair

Expert watchmakers providing comprehensive repair services — from movement overhauls and crystal replacement to water resistance restoration. All work backed by our service guarantee.

Watch Appraisal

Professional valuation services for insurance, estate, and resale purposes. Detailed documentation of condition, authenticity, and market value by certified specialists.

Customer Service

Dedicated support for every aspect of your horological journey. From purchase consultations to after-sale care, our team is committed to your complete satisfaction.
